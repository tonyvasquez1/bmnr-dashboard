Why are the two mNAV values different? The answer is share count. BMNR stopped publishing its share count in weekly press releases after August 2025. The ~455M figure comes from press coverage (Apr 2026). Official count pending Apr 29 10-Q. bmnr.rocks runs a predictive model: they track every ETH purchase, estimate how much capital was raised via ATM equity offerings, and calculate how many new shares were likely issued to fund those purchases. Their model estimates ~530M shares, meaning more dilution has occurred than officially reported. Higher shares → lower NAV/share → higher mNAV multiple. The truth is between the two until the next 10-Q filing confirms the official count.
